About

Scale with an owner’s mentality.

Hank lives in the Maidens / Richmond, Virginia area. He holds a B.S. in Small Business & Entrepreneurship from Virginia Commonwealth University, plus EMT-B and EMS Officer I credentials.

The through-line is growth: start something, grow it, and build the machinery — software, brand image, and public-sector relationships — that holds the next multiple. At Hospital to Home / H2H Emergency Services he built and led Hospital to Home into an enterprise medical-transport organization across Virginia: about 54× revenue over 4.5 years (~140% average annual growth), one location to eight, a workforce from 30 to 975. That work includes Power BI, proprietary software, IT and AI routing and agents, a ~170-vehicle operation, M&A and lender support, and government relations with municipal EMS and Fire, Medicaid, Emergency Planning, and the Virginia Department of Health.

Earlier: physician and provider relations at HCA Capital Division, then a sales-led build at James River Home Health & Hospice / MED Inc Respiratory — $250K to $6M, top-three in the Richmond area, insurance contracting, and the commercial brand of a startup.
